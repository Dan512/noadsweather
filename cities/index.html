Weather by City

Ad-free forecasts for cities around the world — no tracking, no cookies, no clutter. Don't see your city? Search for it.

NoAdsWeather is free and open source, built on a simple idea: checking the forecast shouldn't mean wading through autoplay video, cookie banners, and a dozen tracking scripts. Each city page below has current conditions, hourly and 10-day forecasts, radar, and (for most cities) local climate averages and records going back decades.

It's part of a small family of ad-free tools, alongside pretty cool things like the ability to Find Duplicate Photos Online. If you'd rather the web had more of this and fewer popups, you can support the project on Ko-fi (or don't, I'm not the boss of you).
